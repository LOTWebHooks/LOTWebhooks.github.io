Kamran Saifullah Khan, a cybersecurity researcher, author, and international speaker from Qatar. His work focuses on uncovering security vulnerabilities, exploring emerging attack techniques, and challenging conventional approaches to cybersecurity.

Kamran has contributed to responsible vulnerability disclosure, with acknowledgements from major technology organizations, and has shared his research at international cybersecurity conferences, including Black Hat MEA. His research explores areas such as covert communication channels, data exfiltration techniques, and the evolving methods threat actors use to bypass traditional security defenses.

Beyond research, Kamran is passionate about knowledge sharing, mentoring aspiring security professionals, and contributing to the wider cybersecurity community. He believes that meaningful security research comes from questioning assumptions, exploring overlooked attack surfaces, and turning complex technical findings into practical insights.
